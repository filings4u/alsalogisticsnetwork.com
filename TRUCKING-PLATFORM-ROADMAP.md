# ALSA Logistics Network — trucking platform roadmap

## Baseline assessment (October 2026)
Static GitHub Pages site with service and equipment pages, brochure-style shipper and career pages, demo freight quote and carrier intake forms. Existing forms are *not* connected to a backend. Preserve current navy/blue brand and responsive shell.

## Phase 1 — Conversion pathways
- Freight quote: pickup/drop-off ZIP/city, dates, freight type, weight, dimensions, equipment, hazmat and special requirements, contact data; confirmation/reference number generated by backend
- Shipper onboarding: legal business details, billing and shipping contacts, trade terms acceptance, credit-review request (separate secure process), account review statuses
- Driver recruiting: company-driver/owner-operator differentiation, CDL class, experience, availability, preferred lanes; staged secure application follow-up
- Carrier onboarding: DOT/MC authority, equipment, coverage, insurance verification, tax documentation via private storage
- Separate shipper, driver and carrier CTAs in navigation and homepage

## Phase 2 — Secure backend
Suggested Supabase tables: freight_quote_requests, shippers, shipper_contacts, driver_applications, carrier_applications, onboarding_documents, status_events, audit_logs. Use Supabase Auth for account access, RLS, admin roles, server-side validation, rate limiting/CAPTCHA, notification queue and transactional email. Never store driver license IDs, SSNs, medical/medical-certification details, W-9 data, or insurance attachments in public tables/buckets.

## Phase 3 — Operations dashboards
Shipper portal: quote status, load tracking, document history, shipment requests and invoices.
Driver portal: application status, assignment updates, secure paperwork.
Carrier portal: onboarding status, load offers, proof of delivery and invoices.
Admin console: lead queue, verification checklist, routing, document access, correspondence and analytics.

## Phase 4 — Integration
Connect a TMS and tracking provider; quote automation only after pricing rules or carrier rates are available. Verify legal authority, insurance and applicable FMCSA/employment compliance requirements before collecting regulated applicant information or marketing services.

## Launch gates
No public forms should imply submission is complete until a backend securely persists the record and returns its server-generated ID. Add published privacy policy, retention and consent notices, security tests, spam controls, accessibility audits and end-to-end tests. Avoid unsupported fleet size, authority, service level or capacity claims.
