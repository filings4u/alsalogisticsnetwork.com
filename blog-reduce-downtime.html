Equipment Choice and Shipment Execution

Why the trailer decision comes before the rate

Operational PerspectiveTransportation decisions are strongest when they start with complete freight information and realistic operating requirements.

Trailer selection affects how freight loads, how it secures, where it can travel, and whether the receiving location can unload it. The right equipment plan starts with the physical shipment—not just the lane.

Key planning points

  • Confirm weight and dimensions
  • Understand how the freight will be loaded and unloaded
  • Check site access and dock constraints
  • Identify weather protection or temperature requirements
  • Plan permits or specialized equipment early when needed

Keep the shipment requirements in the center.

Price matters, but a transportation plan also needs the right equipment, timing, communication, and operating fit. ALSA evaluates these factors together when building a trucking or brokerage solution.
