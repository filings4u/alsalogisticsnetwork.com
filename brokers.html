When brokerage makes sense

Use the network when the shipment needs more choices.

Brokerage can support overflow, new lanes, seasonal surges, specialized equipment, project freight, and time-sensitive requirements without forcing a shipper into a rigid transportation model.

  • Seasonal volume
  • New or irregular lanes

  • Multi-stop shipments
