About ALSA Logistics

Who we are.

ALSA Logistics Network LLC was founded with one goal: help trucking businesses become more profitable through professional dispatch services.

Your Success. Our Dispatch. One Team.We become part of your team so you can spend more time driving and less time chasing loads and paperwork.
Who We Are

A dispatch partner focused on your business.

We understand the challenges owner-operators face every day. Finding quality freight, negotiating rates, handling paperwork, and communicating with brokers takes valuable time away from driving. That's where we come in.

Our dispatchers work to keep your trucks loaded with quality freight while treating your business as if it were our own. Whether you own one truck or an entire fleet, we are committed to helping you grow.

MissionTo become one of America's most trusted truck dispatch companies through honest service, excellent communication, and profitable freight opportunities.
VisionTo empower owner-operators and small trucking companies with dependable dispatch solutions that increase profitability and create long-term business success.
